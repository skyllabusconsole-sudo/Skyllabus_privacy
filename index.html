About SKYLLABUS

SKYLLABUS is operated by SKYLLABUS FZ-LLC, United Arab Emirates. This policy covers our website, mobile apps, educational content, wallet and referral features.

01 Introduction

SKYLLABUS FZ-LLC (“SKYLLABUS”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect personal information when you use our applications, website, courses, referral programs, digital wallet features, and related services.

By using SKYLLABUS, you acknowledge the practices described here. Where applicable law requires consent, we will request it separately.

02 Information We Collect

Account and personal information

  • Name, email address, phone number, account credentials and profile details.
  • Referral codes and referral relationships.

Educational information

  • Course enrollments, viewing progress, assessments, results, certificates and course completion records.

Payments and withdrawals

  • Purchases, payment status, transaction IDs, amounts, invoices, refunds, wallet balances and withdrawal requests.
  • Bank account or UPI details supplied for eligible withdrawals.

Technical and support information

  • Device type, operating system, app version, IP address, diagnostic logs and usage information.
  • Support messages, screenshots and attachments you choose to submit.

03 How We Use Information

We use information to register and authenticate users; deliver courses; track learning progress; process purchases, invoices, refunds, referral rewards and withdrawals; provide support; protect against fraud; maintain and improve the service; send essential notifications; and comply with legal obligations.

Promotional communications are sent only where permitted by applicable law and consent requirements.

04 Payments, Wallets & Referrals

We process transaction and beneficiary details to verify purchases, calculate eligible rewards, maintain financial records and facilitate requested withdrawals. Relevant information may be shared with authorized banks, payment processors and payout providers to complete transactions. Rewards and withdrawals remain subject to applicable eligibility and program terms.

05 Information Sharing

We may share information as necessary with payment processors (including Stripe), payout and banking partners, cloud hosting and database providers, authentication and communication services, technical support vendors, and authorities where legally required.

We do not sell personal information to data brokers or third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

06 Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, such as access controls, authentication, secure communications and restricted administrative access. No electronic transmission or storage system is completely secure.

07 Data Retention

We retain information as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain accounts, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and meet accounting, tax and legal obligations. When no longer required, data is deleted or anonymized as appropriate. Some transaction and compliance records may need to be retained after account deletion where permitted or required by law.

08 Account & Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your SKYLLABUS account and associated personal information through the deletion option in the app or the website's account deletion request process.

We may verify account ownership before acting on a request. Following verification, we delete or anonymize eligible personal information, except records we must retain for legal compliance, fraud prevention, unresolved transactions or other permitted reasons.

Account deletion: skyllabus.online/delete-account (publish and activate this page before using this link in Google Play Console).

09 App Permissions

When supported features require it, the app may request access to photos, files or the camera for profile images, support screenshots or attachments. You can manage permissions in your device settings.

10 Children's Privacy

Where our services are available to minors, we apply applicable age-related safeguards and obtain parent or guardian authorization when legally required. We do not knowingly collect children's information in violation of applicable law. Parents or guardians may contact us about applicable access or deletion requests.

11 Third-Party Services

We may use external payment, hosting, video delivery, authentication and technical service providers. These providers may process data under their own privacy policies and contractual obligations. See Stripe's Privacy Policy for information about Stripe.

12 International Data Transfers

As a UAE-based service serving users in other countries, information may be processed or stored internationally. Where required, we apply safeguards under applicable data protection laws.

13 Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of your information, withdraw consent where applicable, object to or restrict certain processing, and request information about our handling of your data. Contact us using the details below.

14 Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our services, legal requirements or information practices. We will publish the revised policy with an updated date and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required.
